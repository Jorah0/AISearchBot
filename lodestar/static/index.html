    Autopilot
    
    
    Off by default. When on, the monitor loop moves each job along its pipeline (Board drawer > Pipeline): steps set to auto run within the AI budgets below; the others ask once per job in the Inbox. Nothing is sent, submitted or moved without its own approval, a stale step is never redone on its own, and eligibility or EEO answers are never drafted. Jobs whose fit check says skip are left alone.
    
    
    
      
      
      
    
    
    
    
    
    
    every step run: by you, Autopilot or an approval, with its outcome and AI calls (also lodestar pipeline log)
    
    
    
  
    Job sources
    
    
    Where Jobs searches and saved searches look. Keyless boards always run; Adzuna and USAJOBS run once you add their free keys here (stored in the OS keyring, checked with one small search). Saved searches ask each board at most once per window (Remotive every 6 hours for all your searches together), so Lodestar stays inside each board's published limits. Listings from Adzuna, Himalayas and We Work Remotely always show the source's name as a link: their terms ask for that credit. LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor and ZipRecruiter are never fetched (their terms forbid it).
    
    
    
  
    Job alerts
    
    
    Subscribe to job alerts on LinkedIn (Jobs > search > Set alert), Indeed, Glassdoor or ZipRecruiter, delivered to the mailbox you connected under Connect email. The mail monitor reads only your own inbox: an alert counts only when its sender is the site's known alert address and the mail passed DKIM/DMARC, and its jobs go to All jobs (Jobs tab), merged with every other source. Lodestar never opens an alert's links (the sites' terms forbid automated visits, and those links can carry one-click sign-in tokens): a link opens only when you click it. Alert mails are not stored. Reading alert mail is low risk under those sites' terms, but not none: switch it off here if you prefer.
    
    
    
    
    
    
    An alert that cannot be read sends one notice a day; save it as a .eml file and run lodestar email parse-alert FILE.eml to see what Lodestar finds in it.
    
  
    Site accounts
    
    
    Your accounts on employer application sites (a Workday tenant, an iCIMS portal), one per site. When an application asks you to sign in, the apply agent signs in with the saved account, or fills the site's sign-up form with your email and a new strong password and stops: you tick the terms and press Create yourself. Passwords stay in the OS keyring and show only when you press Reveal. Codes and verification links come from your connected inbox: used when you click Fill code or Open link, or by themselves on a site where you tick "use my inbox". A CAPTCHA is always yours. Never for LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter or SimplyHired.

    New accounts use this password instead of a generated one, and a site that already knows your email gets one sign-in try with it. It lives only in the OS keyring. One password on many sites means a leak on one site opens the others.

    Browser extension
    
      
    Sends the posting on your tab to the Board and fills application forms; it never clicks, submits or ticks anything. Install: edge://extensions or chrome://extensions > Developer mode > Load unpacked > the extension folder of your Lodestar install. Not for LinkedIn: its User Agreement bans browser add-ons that scrape or copy it, so capture and fill do nothing there.
    
      
    
      
    
      
    
      
    Form answers come from your answer bank (Answers tab), never your Preferences or anything marked private. EEO answers: save them under EEO: gender, EEO: race, EEO: hispanic or latino, EEO: veteran status, EEO: disability status; they go only when you tick "include EEO answers" in the extension. Desired salary: an answer to Desired salary, sent only when you tick it; it must not be your floor. Radio buttons and dropdown widgets stay yours to click.

    Data
    
      
    
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
    
      
    Nightly backups keep the last 7 zips (tool backup). Exports are a JSON dump of every table, or one CSV per table.
    
      
    Restore from backup
    
      
    
        
    
      
    
      
    Puts your data folder back to the zip you pick from the backups folder (Open backups to add one). A safety backup of what is there now is taken first, and the backup's database must open and pass its checks before anything changes. Refused while the monitor loop is mid-cycle, mail is being sent or scanned, an update runs, or another Lodestar window or loop is open. Keyring secrets (AI keys, mail sign-in) and paired browsers are not in backups and stay as they are. Same as lodestar restore ZIP.
    
      
    AI changes
    
      
    Every change Apply suggestions and Full AI made to your content, newest first, with what it was before. Undo puts a batch back exactly; a field you changed since keeps your version.
    
      
    
    
    Audit log
    
      
    Every decision the policy made about an automatic action (allowed, waits for you, blocked) with its reasons, and every action that ran, newest first. Kept for good: the log cannot be edited.

    Update log
    
      
    The monitor loop checks on this interval. An update comes only from the configured origin, fast-forward only, never over your own changes or while mail is being sent or scanned: a backup first, then the tests and lodestar doctor on the new code, rolled back if anything fails. Same as lodestar update.
